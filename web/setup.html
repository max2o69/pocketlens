POCKETLENS
One-time setup

Let Safari use your camera.

Your laptop hosts a private HTTPS page. Safari needs to trust its certificate before it can access the iPhone camera.

Download local certificate
  1. Tap the download button, then allow the profile download.
  2. Open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Choose PocketLens — local HTTPS, tap Install, and enter your iPhone passcode on your phone if prompted.
  3. Open Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings. Enable full trust for PocketLens Local CA.
  4. Return to your laptop dashboard and scan the first pairing QR code. Tap Start camera and allow camera access.
This profile installs a certificate authority generated on your laptop. It grants certificate trust; it does not enroll your phone in device management. Only install it from your own laptop on a trusted network.

When you stop using PocketLens, remove its profile from Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Apple’s certificate trust guide.
