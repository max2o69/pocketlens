Check your browser's webcam access.

This page opens PocketLens through the same browser camera API used by web meeting apps. Pair your phone first to check its live image.

Ready to test. No camera is open.

If you have not granted camera permission, the browser may briefly open its default camera to list device names. This test then selects PocketLens automatically. No microphone access is requested.

Using Chrome for a web meeting

  1. Keep the receiver and your phone camera running.
  2. Allow camera access on your meeting website.
  3. Choose PocketLens in that website's camera selector. Your laptop or headset supplies audio.
  4. Hold up text to check orientation, then make a practice call.

If the device is missing, run Install PocketLens System Camera.cmd and restart Chrome. A successful test here confirms browser capture; the meeting website has its own permissions and camera settings.
